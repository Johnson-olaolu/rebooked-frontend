Round 1 · Auth & onboarding

Every book deserves a second reader.

Assumptions

Mixed audience — students, general readers, local swappers, collectors — so copy stays neutral rather than campus-specific. Desktop first with mobile parity. No payments anywhere in the product, so trust language starts at sign-up, not checkout.

Design language

Old paper, ink and leather. Newsreader for anything that speaks, Karla for anything you operate. Your logo recolored to ink-brown; the open-book drawing reused as a quiet motif.

In this round

Landing, sign up, log in, forgot & reset password, email verification, and a two-step welcome — plus mobile versions of the three screens a phone user hits first. Dashboard, store and messaging come next.
